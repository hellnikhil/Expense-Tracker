Small steps for your money

General ideas to help you build steady habits
💡

Review spending weekly

Use your expense list to spot repeat costs and decide what you want to adjust next week.

Give savings a target

Choose a clear amount and date for a goal, then set aside a manageable amount regularly.

Build a safety buffer

Build emergency savings little by little. RBI suggests working toward enough to cover at least three months of expenses, kept somewhere you can access easily.

Invest with a plan

Choose investments based on what you are saving for, when you will need the money, and how much risk you can handle. Spread your money across different types of investments, and remember their value can go down.

Learn more: RBI emergency fund guidance · SEBI budgeting and goal setting · SEBI investing basics. For education only; this is not personal investment advice.
